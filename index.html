About the book

Principles and Practices of Model Risk Management provides an end-to-end framework for designing, operating, and improving an MRM program aligned with SR 11-7. It covers the three lines of defense and the full model lifecycle—from model development to use, performance monitoring, remediation, and decommissioning.

Key topics

  • SR 11-7 compliance and governance expectations
  • Three lines of defense for MRM
  • Model lifecycle (“cradle to grave”)
  • Model development standards
  • Effective challenge and independent model validation
  • Model governance and control framework
  • Model use, outcomes analysis, and performance monitoring
  • Model risk identification, remediation, and compensating controls
  • Model inventory, taxonomy, and Model Identification Tool
  • AI/ML and generative AI model risk management
