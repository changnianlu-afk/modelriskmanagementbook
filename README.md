# Principles and Practices of Model Risk Management

**Authors:** Charlie Lu and Peter Russo

Official webpage and resources for the book *Principles and Practices of Model Risk Management*.

## About the book
*Principles and Practices of Model Risk Management* is a practical textbook for financial engineering students and model risk practitioners. It provides an end-to-end framework for building, operating, and improving an MRM program aligned with **SR 11-7**, including the role of the **three lines of defense**.

The book follows the **model lifecycle from cradle to grave**, covering model development standards, **effective challenge and independent model validation**, **governance and control**, model use, and **performance monitoring**. It also addresses **model risk identification**, issue management, **remediation and compensating controls**, and the operating infrastructure needed for scale—such as **model inventory management** and a **Model Identification Tool** to distinguish models from non-models. Finally, it extends the framework to **AI/ML and generative AI model risk management**, highlighting key risks and controls unique to modern AI systems.

Case studies include the **London Whale**, **LTCM**, and the **subprime CDO** crisis.
